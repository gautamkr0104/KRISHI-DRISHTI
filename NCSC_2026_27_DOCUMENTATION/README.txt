SCIENTIFIC HONESTY DEFENSE REMINDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When judges ask tough questions, remember:
1. Pathogen Match != Exact Classification: In our 6-class model, Potato Late Blight
   and Tomato Late Blight are distinct classes. If cross-predicted, the underlying
   Phytophthora infestans oomycete biology matches, but it is not an exact 6-class match.
2. Optical Shroud is an Image Acquisition Tool: It enforces an 8–10 cm focal distance
   and diffuses shadows, but it cannot prevent an alien green leaf (like tea) from
   registering false confidence in a closed-set model. Multi-modal gating is essential.
3. DHT11 Microclimate Telemetry: Instantaneous RH < 85% is a simplified agronomic risk
   advisory based on Wallin/Hyre models; it suppresses unnecessary chemical spraying,
   but does not biologically prove that spores are absent.
4. Next Major Milestone: Physical field validation with genuine captures taken by the
   wand in local agricultural plots across Bihar.
