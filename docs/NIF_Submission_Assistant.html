TITLE OF INNOVATION "AN OFFLINE-FIRST DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR PREDICTIVE PLANT HEALTH SCREENING AND MICROCLIMATE TELEMETRY (KRISHI-DRISHTI)" 3. GENESIS & AGRICULTURAL PROBLEM ADDRESSED In Indian agriculture—particularly across potato and tomato farming in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, and Punjab—foliar blights (Phytophthora infestans and Alternaria solani) destroy 20% to 35% of annual crop yield. Existing diagnostic solutions fail Indian smallholder farmers due to excessive cost (optical kits cost ₹25,000–₹60,000), absence of rural cellular internet, and reactive rather than predictive operation. KRISHI-DRISHTI is engineered as an integrated, handheld, zero-cloud agricultural screening system manufactured under ₹1,500 INR ($18 USD) that operates 100% offline without cellular service, cloud servers, or subscriptions. 4. TECHNICAL SOLUTION & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE The system operates as an integrated Hardware-Software Triad over a private, ad-hoc 2.4 GHz local Wi-Fi mesh: 1. The Optical Capture Node (AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM): 5MP OV5640 sensor, SoftAP (192.168.4.1), 8GB MicroSD in 1-bit SD_MMC DMA mode with frame verification (written >= 512 bytes). 2. The Handheld Telemetry Wand (ESP32 DevKit V1): SSD1306 128x64 I2C OLED display, DHT11 canopy microclimate sensor, FreeRTOS non-blocking polling state machine with stale scan rejection. 3. The Offline Pocket AI Co-Processor: Runs an optimized MobileNetV3 Float16 neural model locally on Android RAM with 10.54 ms inference latency per leaf. 5. SCIENTIFIC NOVELTY: THE PLANT DISEASE TRIANGLE KRISHI-DRISHTI correlates foliar computer vision with microclimate risk envelopes: • Late Blight Envelope: Canopy RH > 85% with 18°C–24.5°C temperature sustained. • Early Blight Envelope: 24°C–29°C with fluctuating moisture. • Information Entropy Gating (H = -sum(p * ln(p))): If H > 1.45 nats or max confidence < 50%, flags UNCERTAIN, rejecting false positive diagnoses on non-target leaves. 6. EMPIRICAL VERIFICATION • Training Convergence Accuracy: 91.80% • Validation Accuracy: 88.74% (evaluated on 2,850 unseen real leaf photographs) • Validation Macro F1 Score: 0.8863 • Edge Inference Latency: 10.54 ms (CPU) • Edge Model Size: 2.01 MB (Float16 Post-Training Quantization) • Firmware Verification: Exit Code 0 (arduino-cli) for both nodes • Automated Test Suite: 6/6 tests passing (100% OK) 7. PROPOSED NOVEL PATENT CLAIMS FOR FREE FILING 1. Apparatus Claim: An offline-first, distributed agricultural screening triad comprising a handheld environmental telemetry wand, an asynchronous optical capture unit, and a localized edge co-processor communicating over an ad-hoc local mesh without internet connectivity. 2. Method Claim: A method for predictive plant disease screening correlating ambient canopy temperature and humidity thresholds with real-time foliar Shannon information entropy and neural classification. 3. Firmware Decoupling Method: A non-blocking telemetry protocol where high-resolution frame capture, local wireless dispatches, pocket neural inference, and OLED carousel rendering are asynchronously decoupled across independent microcontrollers. 8.
