    Problem Statement & Agronomic Reality

    The Rural Foliar Crisis: Why Smallholders Suffer

    Catastrophic crop losses, chemical debt spirals, and the digital divide in rural Indian agriculture

    🥀
    1. ECONOMIC COLLAPSE
  • 40%–70% Yield Losses: Tomato Early Blight (Alternaria solani) and Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) decimate entire harvests within 72 hours.
  • Devastating Input Debt: Small farmers invest ₹30,000–₹40,000/acre in seed and fertilizer. A single unspotted fungal bloom triggers total bankruptcy.
  • Pesticide Overuse: Blind application of broad-spectrum fungicides up to 14 times per season pollutes local soil ecology and water tables.
    • 🏥
    2. DIAGNOSTIC DESERT
  • Zero Pathologist Access: In rural districts like Begusarai, certified agricultural officers and plant pathology clinics are virtually nonexistent on-site.
  • Commercial Labs Unaffordable: Commercial tissue testing costs ₹1,500–₹3,000/sample and takes 7–10 days—far too late to save standing crops.
  • Dealer Misinformation: Farmers are forced to rely on informal advice from commercial pesticide retailers, compounding expenses.
    • 📵
    3. CONNECTIVITY VOID
  • Cloud Apps Fail in Fields: Commercial smartphone ag-apps depend on continuous 4G/5G internet, which regularly drops to zero in open Indian farms.
  • Delicate Phone Vulnerability: Farmers cannot risk dropping expensive glass smartphones in wet mud, irrigation ditches, and pesticide spray.
  • The Need: A rugged, handheld, dedicated wand that provides instant optical screening with 100% offline edge processing.
    • Scientific Principles & Theoretical Framework

    Multi-Disciplinary Scientific Foundation

    Merging plant pathology, digital optoelectronics, embedded microcontroller architectures, and convolutional machine vision

    🔬
    PLANT PATHOLOGY & SYMPTOMATOLOGY
  • Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Fungal pathogen secreting alternaric acid phytotoxins. Forms dark, concentric target-board rings with distinct yellow chlorotic halos.
  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Aggressive oomycete causing water-soaked, irregular lesions that expand rapidly under cool, humid conditions (RH > 85%, 18°–24.5°C).
  • Disease Triangle Modeling: Evaluates host susceptibility, pathogen presence, and microclimate envelope simultaneously.
    • 📸
    OPTOELECTRONICS & CMOS SENSING
  • Photoelectric Conversion: The OV5640 5MP CMOS active pixel sensor converts reflected leaf photon flux into discrete electrical charges.
  • Bayer Color Matrix: Incident spectrum passes through an RGGB color mosaic, digitized internally by high-speed ADCs into compressed 1600×1200 UXGA JPEG frames.
  • Macro Focal Calibration: Calibrated at 10–12 cm focal length to resolve foliar lesion micro-structures down to 1.5 mm diameter.
    • ⚡
    EMBEDDED COMPUTING & DMA
  • Dual-Core Xtensa Architecture: ESP32 operates at 240 MHz, executing Wi-Fi networking, sensor telemetry, and camera control concurrently.
  • PSRAM Direct Memory Access: Streams massive 1600×1200 frame buffers directly into 4 MB external PSRAM without starving internal SRAM.
  • Non-Volatile Logging: Serializes captured diagnostic evidence over High-Speed SPI to a FAT32 MicroSD card.
    • 🧠
    CONVOLUTIONAL NEURAL NETWORKS (CNN)
  • Hierarchical Feature Extraction: Deep 2D convolutional kernels learn spatial edge gradients, lesion margin contours, and necrotic surface textures.
  • Depthwise Separable Convolutions: Slashes computational FLOPs by 85% compared to standard CNNs, enabling real-time edge execution on phone CPUs.
  • Softmax Probability Distribution: Outputs normalized confidence scores across all 6 Solanaceae target classes.

  • Host: Any standard farmer Android phone

  • Model: TFLite Float16 (2.01 MB)

    Artificial Intelligence Engine

    MobileNetV3-Small Deep Learning Pipeline

    Trained on 14,249 Solanaceae images with 2-stage transfer learning and Float16 Post-Training Quantization

    ⚙️
    TRAINING PROTOCOL & HYPERPARAMETERS
  • Neural Backbone: MobileNetV3-Small initialized with ImageNet-1k weights.
  • Input Tensor: 128 × 128 × 3 RGB image, scaled to [-1, 1] range.
  • Stage 1 Warmup: Dense classification head trained (Adam lr = 1e-3, 5 epochs) with frozen convolutional backbone.
  • Stage 2 Deep Fine-Tuning: Top 25 convolutional layers unfrozen with cosine learning rate decay (lr = 1e-4) to learn delicate lesion margins.
  • Post-Training Quantization (PTQ): Float16 quantization reducing weights from FP32 to 16-bit floats.
  • Model Artifact Size: Exactly 2,104,584 bytes (2.01 MB)—instantly cached in phone RAM.
    • 🗂️
    CORPUS & STRICT 1:1 TAXONOMY
  • Primary Corpus: New Plant Diseases Dataset (Augmented 87K, Solanaceae Partition: 14,249 images).
  • Training Set: 11,399 images (80%) | Validation Set: 2,850 strictly unseen images (20%).

    Diagnostic Safeguards & AI Safety

    Zero-Hallucination Uncertainty Gating

    Ensuring agricultural recommendations are scientifically defensible and never invent fake diagnoses

    ⚖️
    SHANNON ENTROPY REJECTION
  • The Core AI Dilemma: Standard neural classifiers blindly output whichever class has the highest score—even if presented with an out-of-focus blur or dark shadow.
  • Mathematical Entropy Gating:
    H = - Σ (p_i · ln p_i) [for i = 1 to 6]
  • Rejection Criteria: If $H > 1.45$ nats (high diagnostic ambiguity) OR top probability $p_{max} < 0.50$, the inference engine triggers UNCERTAIN.
  • Operator Instruction: The OLED wand prompts the farmer: 'Hold wand steady at 10 cm, avoid shadow, and re-scan.'
    • 🛡️
    FOLIAR PLAUSIBILITY & HONESTY
  • Non-Leaf Edge Filter: Frames with < 12% chlorophyll or necrotic lesion coverage automatically reject lens caps, bare ground, fingers, or walls as NON_LEAF.
  • Hardware Verification: Physical hardware testing verified that the OV5640 camera reliably captures 1600×1200 JPEGs to the MicroSD card, and the OLED carousel updates properly.
  • Authentic Scope: We do not claim full commercial multi-farm field trials have occurred. The Android inference pipeline is currently a verified laboratory prototype.
  • Triage Tool Only: Digital screening aids early intervention; it does not replace laboratory agronomist confirmations.

    Embedded Engineering & Workmanship

    Dual-Firmware Hardening & Production Reliability

    Hardware watchdog timers, frame-write verification, SDMMC static streaming, and 4-page OLED carousel

    📷
    CAMERA UNIT (KRISHI_DRISHTI_Camera.ino)
  • Write Verification: Strictly evaluates written >= 512 && written == fb->len. Drops corrupt frames with HTTP 500 without incrementing the scan index.
  • SDMMC Static Asset Streaming: Custom handleStaticFile() with CORS headers streams vendor libraries, CSS, and the 2.01 MB TFLite model directly from the 8GB MicroSD card.
  • Embedded HTTP Daemon: Serves the self-contained offline browser UI so any farmer phone connects to SoftAP and runs AI without downloading an app.
  • Compilation Footprint: Flash: 1,159,119 bytes (36%) | RAM: 60,172 bytes (18%) | Exit Code 0.
    • 🪄
    CONTROLLER WAND (KRISHI_DRISHTI_Controller.ino)
  • Non-Blocking Station Link: Connects to SoftAP 'KRISHI-DRISHTI' (192.168.4.1) with an asynchronous state machine for resilient reconnects.
  • Watchdog & Stale Scan Rejection: Validates retScanId == activeScanId with a 14-second watchdog timeout to prevent displaying stale results.
  • 4-Page OLED Carousel: (1) System & Disease Name | (2) Microclimate Risk Index | (3) Biocontrol Advisory | (4) Chemical Prescription.
  • DHT11 Microclimate Envelope: Triggers high disease risk warning when RH > 85% and Temp is between 18.0°C and 24.5°C.
